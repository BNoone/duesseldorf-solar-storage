About this page

What this models

A thought experiment: every suitable roof in Duesseldorf carries solar, feeding storage. It is not a forecast, and it is not a plan anyone has proposed.

What counts as a suitable roof

A building qualifies once its roof faces sum to at least 10 kW. North-facing pitched faces are excluded. Flat roofs count, since panels on them are angled south regardless of the roof's raw tilt.

Where the numbers come from

Roof potential: Solarkataster NRW. What is already installed: the Marktstammdatenregister, Germany's renewable energy registry. Weather, for the heatwave comparison: ERA5, via Open-Meteo.

The cooling-demand figure

The cooling-demand toggle uses a France analogue: French evening electricity demand ran 25% above off-season levels during the 2025 heatwaves (IEA). German residential air conditioning ownership is too low for a domestic figure to exist, so this is the closest real comparison available, never a Duesseldorf measurement.
